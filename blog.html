Update
We've officially started production.

We’re excited to share our first major milestone.

Over the past few months, OverPick has existed as an idea, a set of rules, a lot of whiteboard sketches, and many late-night conversations about sports, strategy, and competition. Today, it becomes something more tangible. We have officially begun production on the OverPick app.


Our goal is simple: create a head-to-head sports strategy experience where your understanding of matchups, usage, and game flow actually matters. Instead of just watching a game and reacting to what happens, OverPick lets you make decisions beforehand and compete directly against another person based on how well you read the sport.
